Interactive Activities

💬  1 · Slack Workspace

We have created a Slack workspace for authors and attendees interested in the workshop topics to discuss ideas and share papers prior to the workshop date.

All accepted papers present posters during the 3:30 – 4:15 PM session, with coffee served alongside. Authors stay at their boards for the full slot so attendees can work through methods and results one-on-one with them.

We will collect questions for the panel discussion from the Slack workspace, an online poll, and in-person attendees.
